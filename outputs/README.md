# Historical runs

These exploratory predictions, fitted models and reports are retained for
traceability. Blend weights were selected on the reported out-of-fold targets;
interval coverage summaries reuse calibration observations. They are superseded
by the project-held-out results in `results/revised_stress/` and
`results/revised_intervals/`.

The serialized models use historical preprocessing and are not revised model
exports. Do not apply revised stress variables to them. Historical SHAP,
boosting and sensitivity plots do not establish performance of the revision.
